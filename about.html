History

Sezibera Construction is a Rwandan building contractor delivering residential and commercial projects from groundbreaking through to handover. We are a builder first: the structure, the envelope and the finishes are all carried out by our own team.

Construction goes wrong in the gaps — between trades, between drawings and what actually gets built, between who promised a date and who has to meet it. We built this company to close those gaps by keeping the work under one team that answers for all of it.

That means our people are on site while the work goes in, not reviewing it afterwards. It means when something is wrong, we fix it rather than explain it. And it means one point of accountability for the client, from the first setting-out peg to the final snag.
