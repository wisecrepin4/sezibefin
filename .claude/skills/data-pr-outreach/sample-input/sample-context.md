# Data PR context

This is a sample input for the Data PR and Outreach skill. Provide the skill with your data file (see `sample-transactions.csv`) plus a short context note like this one.

---

**Business:** Tidewater Outdoors, an Australian online retailer selling camping, hiking, fishing and 4WD recovery gear. We ship Australia-wide from a warehouse in Geelong, Victoria.

**Industry:** Outdoor and adventure retail, ecommerce.

**Target audience:** Australian adults who camp, hike, fish, or four-wheel-drive. A good share of our customers live outside the capital cities.

**Geographic market:** National.

**The data:** Two years of transaction records, January 2024 to December 2025. Each row is one order, with the customer's region (metro or regional), state, product category, order total, item count, and whether the customer was new or returning. We have only ever used this data for inventory planning. We have never looked at it for anything else.

**Target journalists:** These are sample journalists for the demo. Swap in real ones before you send anything. For each, the skill needs a recent article and what it was about.

1. **Priya Nadarajah, Retail Pulse AU.** Sample recent article: "Why the cost-of-living squeeze is reshaping where Australians shop", a piece on how household budgets are changing consumer behaviour in retail.

2. **Tom Castellano, SmartCommerce Weekly.** Sample recent article: "Ecommerce delivery costs are quietly killing regional sales", an opinion piece arguing online retailers under-serve customers outside the major cities.

3. **Bec Holloway, The Regional Business Review.** Sample recent article: "Country towns are outgrowing the 'small market' label", a feature on the rising economic weight of regional Australia.

**Anything else:** We would love national coverage but we are realistic. Trade and regional press are fine targets too. We can offer the full anonymised data set and an interview with our founder.
