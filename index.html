Full-service building contractor for your future home.

Headquartered in Rwanda, Sezibera Construction delivers residential and commercial buildings from groundbreaking through to handover. We bring the same care to every building we construct, no matter the size.

We keep structural work, site logistics and finishing trades under one team, so quality and programme stay in our control rather than scattered across subcontractors. Our clients deal with one contractor who carries responsibility for the whole build.
